About us

On Salaama Road, caring for Kampala

A neighbourhood pharmacy built on one idea: real medicine, and a straight answer, without a long drive across town.

Our story

Since 2016, open around the clock

TRESHA Pharmacy has been operating since 2016, starting as a small counter with one goal: make sure neighbours could get genuine medicine and honest advice close to home. That's still the whole idea today — a licensed pharmacist on the floor at every hour we're open, and a shelf that's actually stocked with what your prescription calls for.

We work with vetted, licensed suppliers and keep a close eye on expiry and storage, because what leaves our counter goes straight into your family's medicine cabinet.

Our team

Pharmacists who know your name

Behind the counter is a small team of registered pharmacists and trained assistants who treat every visit as a conversation, not a transaction. Ask a question, compare two medicines, or just double-check a dosage — nobody rushes you.

Every pharmacist on our floor is registered with the Uganda Pharmacy Council.
