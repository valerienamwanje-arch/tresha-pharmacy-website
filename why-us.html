Why choose us

What you get every time you walk in

No surprises at the till, no turned-away prescriptions — just a pharmacy that does the basics properly.

Licensed & verified

Every pharmacist is registered with the Uganda Pharmacy Council, and every medicine is sourced from approved suppliers — nothing informal, nothing unverified.

Open around the clock

Both the pharmacy and Infinity Diagnostics Centre run 24/7 — right across from Kiruddu General Hospital, for whenever care can't wait.

Rarely out of stock

We track fast-moving prescriptions closely, so you're not sent elsewhere for something common.

Private, unhurried advice

Ask a pharmacist a question without an audience — our consultation corner is quiet and judgment-free.

Diagnostics on-site

Skip the referral queue. Infinity Diagnostics Centre handles testing right inside our pharmacy, with results the same day.

Delivery across Kampala

Order by WhatsApp or phone and get medicine at your door, often within hours.

Transparent pricing

What you're quoted is what you pay — no hidden charges at the till.
